About the models

One step = one trading hour; 280 hours = one PoC month = the Delta's approximate horizon.

Users supply annual implied volatility and annual drift in percentage points, automatically converted to trading-hour values using 14 hours/day, 5 days/week, and 52 weeks/year. Volatility is divided by the square root of annual trading hours; drift by annual trading hours. An hourly move is a net change; volatility sets its typical size, drift adds a small bias. Up-and-down behaviour emerges across steps.

Completed grid trades are sampled independently of price movement from the observed trade count and calendar duration. The defaults represent 30 completed buy/sell cycles over 50 calendar days (500 trading hours), or 0.060 completed trades per trading hour. Only the completed cycle (sell) earns Profit per Grid Trade; the buy fill is not counted again.

The Delta PoT is a heuristic snapshot from setup. The Monte Carlo grid runs past one month until a boundary is touched.

Delta PoT and MC first-hit rates measure different things; compare them only as a sanity check.
